## B2

**1.** Joan finds keeping grammatical rules in mind very difficult.

- Palabra clave: `EASY`
- Joan says it ____________ grammatical rules in mind.
- Parte 1: `is not easy`
- Parte 2: `to keep`
- Completa: `is not easy to keep`
- Resuelta: Joan says it is not easy to keep grammatical rules in mind.

**2.** I'm sorry I'm late, said Peter.

- Palabra clave: `APOLOGISED`
- Peter ____________ late.
- Parte 1: `apologised`
- Parte 2: `for being`
- Completa: `apologised for being`
- Resuelta: Peter apologised for being late.

## C1

**1.** I have never studied so hard in my life for an exam.

- Palabra clave: `HAVE`
- Never before ____________ in my life for an exam.
- Parte 1: `have I`
- Parte 2: `studied so hard`
- Completa: `have I studied so hard`
- Resuelta: Never before have I studied so hard in my life for an exam.

**2.** We must take as much advantage as possible of any opportunity to speak English.

- Palabra clave: `MOST`
- We have ____________ any opportunity to speak English.
- Parte 1: `to make`
- Parte 2: `the most of`
- Completa: `to make the most of`
- Resuelta: We have to make the most of any opportunity to speak English.

## C2

**1.** As soon as the tennis match ended, the crowd bursts into cheers.

- Palabra clave: `SOONER`
- No ____________ the crowd burst into cheers.
- Parte 1: `sooner had the tennis match ended`
- Parte 2: `than`
- Completa: `sooner had the tennis match ended than`
- Resuelta: No sooner had the tennis match ended than the crowd burst into cheers.

**2.** I'm sure it was a surprise to everyone when the headteacher resigned suddenly.

- Palabra clave: `BLUE`
- The headteacher's sudden resignation must ____________ everyone.
- Parte 1: `have come`
- Parte 2: `out of the blue to`
- Completa: `have come out of the blue to`
- Resuelta: The headteacher's sudden resignation must have come out of the blue to everyone.
